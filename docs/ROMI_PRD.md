## 1. Business Objective

### 1.1 Primary Business Question

Which marketing investments — by channel, campaign type, product line, and geography — are generating the highest return, and how does actual marketing ROI compare to plan by fiscal period?

---

### 1.2 Current State Pain

Marketing spend is tracked in Allocadia (planning tool). Campaign performance data lives in Salesforce (pipeline and revenue). Channel costs are in separate spreadsheets maintained by channel owners. Finance consolidates these manually every quarter for leadership review — a process that takes 3–4 weeks, is prone to attribution errors, and produces a report that is already stale by the time it is distributed. There is no single source of truth for ROMI at any level of the organization.

---

### 1.3 Decisions Enabled

- Rebalance marketing investment mid-year toward channels and campaigns with the highest return
- Compare ROMI across product lines and business units to inform future budget allocation
- Provide Finance with a reliable, auditable ROMI figure for QBR and board reporting
- Give campaign managers real-time visibility into their spend efficiency before the quarter closes
- Identify underperforming campaigns before the budget is fully consumed

## 3. Data Scope

### 3.1 Primary Grain

**Grain statement:** One row in the fact table represents one **campaign** per **fiscal month** per **channel**.

A campaign is the atomic unit of marketing investment. Channel is included in the grain because a single campaign may run across multiple channels with different spend and performance profiles.

---

### 3.2 Time Range and History

| Dimension | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Historical coverage | 3 fiscal years (FY2024, FY2025, FY2026 to date) |
| Refresh cadence | Weekly — every Monday by 6am |
| Snapshot logic | Week-ending Friday; month-closing snapshot on last business day |
| Fiscal calendar | Company fiscal calendar (Feb–Jan year-end) |

---

### 3.3 Scope Boundaries

**In scope:**
- Paid media campaigns (digital, social, search, programmatic)
- Field events and sponsored conferences (marketing-attributed)
- Content syndication and lead generation programs
- Email marketing programs with measurable pipeline attribution
- Allocadia budget vs. actual by campaign
- Salesforce opportunity and revenue attribution

**Out of scope (explicit exclusions):**
- Brand awareness spend (no measurable attribution — tracked separately)
- PR and analyst relations spend
- Internal marketing tools and technology spend
- Sales-originated pipeline (even if marketing-touched)
- Individual sales rep performance data

## 4. Key Metrics and Measures

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Priority |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **ROMI** | Return on Marketing Investment — net revenue attributable to marketing divided by total marketing spend | `(Attributed Revenue - Marketing Spend) / Marketing Spend` | MVP |
| **Attributed Revenue** | Revenue from closed-won opportunities where marketing is the primary source or a significant influence | Salesforce opportunity close amount × attribution weight | MVP |
| **Total Marketing Spend** | Total cost of running a campaign, including media, creative, events, and agency fees | Sum of all cost line items per campaign per period | MVP |
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** | Total spend divided by qualified leads generated | `Total Spend / Leads Generated` | MVP |
| **Cost Per Opportunity (CPO)** | Total spend divided by opportunities created | `Total Spend / Opportunities Created` | MVP |
| **Pipeline Influenced** | Total pipeline value of opportunities where marketing played a role (not necessarily primary source) | Sum of open opportunity amounts with any marketing touch | MVP |
| **Lead-to-Opportunity Rate** | Percentage of leads that converted to a qualified opportunity | `Opportunities Created / Leads Generated` | MVP |
| **Opportunity-to-Close Rate** | Percentage of opportunities that resulted in closed-won revenue | `Deals Closed Won / Opportunities Created` | MVP |
| **Budget vs. Actual Variance** | Difference between planned spend (Allocadia) and actual spend | `Actual Spend - Planned Spend` | MVP |
| **Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) Velocity** | Rate at which MQLs are being generated per period | `MQLs this period / MQLs prior period` | MVP |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | Total marketing spend divided by new customers acquired | `Total Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired` | Phase 2 |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | Ratio of customer lifetime value to acquisition cost | `LTV / CAC` | Phase 2 |

## 6. Success Criteria

### 6.1 Definition of Done

Marketing Operations can answer the question "Which campaigns delivered the highest ROMI last quarter?" from a single data product — without touching Allocadia, Salesforce, or a spreadsheet. The answer is available by Tuesday of the first week following quarter close.

Finance can reproduce the ROMI figure used in any board presentation from the audit trail in the data product, with full traceability back to the originating opportunity and campaign.

---

## 7. Constraints and Dependencies

### 7.1 Access and Permissions

- **Workday Financials** requires Finance VP approval before data access is granted. Dependency logged.
- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager** API access request submitted to IT. ETA: 2 weeks.
- **Allocadia** API key must be provisioned for the DSA service account. Marketing Ops to coordinate.

---

### 7.2 Compliance Requirements

- **GDPR / Data Privacy:** Lead-level data (name, email, company) must not be surfaced in the ROMI product layer. Individual lead identification is out of scope — aggregate counts only.
- **Revenue data sensitivity:** Individual deal values above $500K are considered commercially sensitive. These must be masked in any role except Finance BP and Marketing Operations.
- **Vendor spend sensitivity:** Spend with named agencies and media vendors is confidential. Do not surface vendor names or individual invoice amounts in Campaign Manager-accessible views.

---

### 7.3 Governance Requirements

| Field | Governance Rule | Roles Affected |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
